# Standard SOC 2 Type II Bridge Letter Template

```text
[COMPANY LETTERHEAD]

Date: [Date of Letter, e.g., October 20, 2026]

To: Prospective Enterprise Customers, Partners, and Auditors
Subject: SOC 2 Type II Bridge Letter for [Company Name, Inc.]

Dear Vendor Security Review Team,

This letter serves to bridge the period between our most recent System and Organization Controls (SOC) 2 Type II examination report and the present date.

1. Background & Audit Scope:
[Company Name, Inc.] engaged [Independent CPA Audit Firm Name] to perform a SOC 2 Type II examination of our [Product/Platform Name] system. The resulting report covered the Trust Services Criteria of Security, Availability, and Confidentiality for the period of [Start Date, e.g., October 1, 2024] to [End Date, e.g., September 30, 2025]. The independent auditor's report was issued on [Report Date] with an unqualified (clean) opinion.

2. Gap Period:
The period covered by this Bridge Letter commences on [Day after previous report, e.g., October 1, 2025] and extends through the date of this letter, [Current Date, e.g., October 20, 2026] ("the Gap Period").

3. Management Confirmation & Negative Assurance:
To the best of our knowledge and belief:
a) [Company Name, Inc.] has maintained continuous, effective operation of all internal controls, security policies, and technical safeguards described in our SOC 2 Type II report throughout the Gap Period.
b) There have been no material changes, modifications, or degradations to our production infrastructure, cloud hosting environments (AWS/GCP), access management controls, or encryption protocols that would adversely affect our ability to meet the Trust Services Criteria.
c) There have been no material security incidents, confirmed unauthorized data access, or breach events affecting customer confidential information during the Gap Period.

4. Next Audit Cycle:
Our next annual SOC 2 Type II examination covering the period [Current Year Window] is currently underway with [Audit Firm Name], with report delivery scheduled for [Expected Date, e.g., December 2026].

Sincerely,

_________________________________________
[Executive Name]
[Title: Chief Technology Officer / Chief Information Security Officer]
[Company Name, Inc.]
[Company Domain / Email]
```
